Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Summary

Lanelings can be played without an account. We do not ask for your name, email or payment details. We only keep anonymous technical data so the game works and to improve it.

What we collect

  • An anonymous player number, randomly generated and stored in your browser or the app. It lets us recognise you when you rejoin a room and group your logs. It does not say who you are.
  • Technical match logs: performance (frames per second, stutters), device data (CPU, memory, graphics card, screen, browser or app), match events (hero, mode, duration) and errors.
  • In-match chat messages, so the bots can reply and to review abuse.
  • Website visits: which page, where you came from (for example, an ad), whether you clicked Play or Download, and the device type and screen size.

What we do not collect

We do not store your IP address: to limit abuse we use an irreversible code derived from it that cannot be turned back into the IP. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell data.

How we use it

To run online rooms (including rejoining), to find and fix bugs and performance problems, to let the bots reply in chat, and to know how many people arrive from each ad.

Who it is shared with

With the services that run the game: Oracle Cloud (online match server), Netlify (log storage and chat replies), Google (Gemini writes the bots’ replies from the chat messages), GitHub (app downloads) and Google Fonts (website fonts).

How long

Logs are kept for at most 12 months and then deleted.

Your choices

You can turn off log sending in the game: Options → "Send match logs to improve the game". Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes your player number.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For any question about your data, write to us with the Feedback button in the game menu.
