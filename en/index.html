The game

What is Lanelings?

A classic MOBA: pick a hero, level up, buy items and fight alongside your team to bring down the enemy nexus. All in pixel art.

  • 5 vs 5

    Three lanes, towers, a jungle and a nexus to defend. Short matches, with bots or friends.

  • With bots or friends

    Practice against bots, or create a room and send the link to your friends. Empty slots are filled with bots.

  • 26 heroes

    Tanks, mages, assassins, marksmen, fighters and supports, each with their own silhouette and four abilities.

  • Original pixel art

    Every hero is modeled in 3D and turned into pixel art frame by frame, in one style across the whole game.

A small game, in active development

Lanelings is made by a very small team. It is early: some things are rough, the balance changes often and there are modes still missing. Your feedback really shapes the game: send it from the “Feedback” button in the game menu.

Lanelings for Windows

The same game, in its own window. Optional: it plays the same in your browser.

  • Uses your graphics card at full power, without browser throttling.
  • Updates itself when a new version comes out.
  • Same rooms as the web version: play with friends who are in the browser.
Download installer

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)

The installer is not digitally signed yet: if Windows shows a blue SmartScreen warning, click “More info” and then “Run anyway”.

On Mac or Linux? Play in your browser on your computer.
